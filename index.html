Chore Jars

Chores made fun for kids, easy for parents. Kids earn money (or points, if you prefer) for completed chores, track their spending, and get paid out — all synced across the family's devices.

Support & FAQ

How do kids join our family?

On the kid's device: open the app → "We have a family code — join" → scan the QR code shown in the parent's ⚙️ settings (or type the code) → tap their name.

The join code says "expired."

The QR/code refreshes weekly for safety. Ask a parent to open ⚙️ settings and share the current code or QR.

A chore isn't showing today.

Chores appear only on their scheduled days (e.g. weekdays only). A parent can check the schedule under ⚙️ settings → Chores.

I forgot the parent PIN.

Any parent already in parent mode can see it: ⚙️ settings → family card → "tap to reveal."

How do I delete our data?

⚙️ settings → Danger zone → "Reset everything" permanently deletes all family data. Or email us and we'll delete it for you.

Can chores pay real money — and how do kids get paid?

Set an amount per chore and/or a weekly allowance in each kid's settings. When they cash out, a parent can hand over cash or send it via Venmo, Cash App, or PayPal, right from the app.

Can we use points or stars instead of money?

Yes — switch any kid to a custom points system in their settings if you'd rather they earn points/stars than real money.

I got a new phone — how do I get our family back?

Sign in with the same Apple or Google account a parent used before, and the app will offer to reconnect you to your family automatically.
